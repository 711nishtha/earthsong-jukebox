## Not compared

- **Storm Drums:** the globe rotates, so how much lightning is on screen in a window depends on which side of Earth faces the camera as much as on the season. A seasonal comparison would be confounded, so we do not offer one.
- **Forest Heights, Four Winds:** single periods (one year of GEDI data; six weeks of aerosols), so there is no meaningful before/after.
